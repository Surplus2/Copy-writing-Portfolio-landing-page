Common Questions

Everything You
Need to Know

How long does a project typically take? +
Most welcome and nurture sequences (5–7 emails) are delivered within 7–10 business days after our discovery call and research phase. Launch campaigns and larger projects may take 2–3 weeks. I'll always give you a clear timeline before we begin.
Do you write for brands outside Nigeria? +
Absolutely. I work with brands across Nigeria and the United States, and I'm comfortable adapting tone and cultural context for both markets. Whether your audience is in Lagos or Los Angeles, the copy will feel native and resonant.
What information do you need to get started? +
After the discovery call, I'll send a short creative brief: your brand voice, target audience, product/service details, past email performance (if any), and your goals. The more context you give me, the sharper the copy.
How many revisions are included? +
Every project includes two rounds of revisions at no extra cost. In practice, most clients are happy after the first draft — but the revisions are there to make sure every word earns its place.
What industries do you specialise in? +
I've written for luxury brands, health & wellness, edtech, coaching, e-commerce, and personal brands. I'm particularly strong in any niche where storytelling and emotion drive the purchase decision — which is most of them.
